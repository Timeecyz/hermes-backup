## 英文脚本框架（已定稿）

**时长：约2分40秒（正常语速+适当停顿）**

```
Hi everyone! Today I want to tell you about a special day in China — the Dragon Boat Festival.

[Part 1 — Who was Qu Yuan?]
More than 2,000 years ago, there lived a great man in China. His name was Qu Yuan.
He was a poet. He was also a minister. He loved his country — the Kingdom of Chu — very much.
He wrote beautiful poems. People still read them today. He was very, very smart.

[Part 2 — Why did he jump into the river?]
One day, something bad happened. The king made some wrong decisions. Qu Yuan tried to help, but the king did not listen.
He was very sad. He was sent away from the capital. He could not save his country.
One morning, Qu Yuan walked to the Miluo River. He was 62 years old. He looked at the water... and he jumped in.

[Part 3 — How do we remember him?]
When people heard, they ran to the river. They got into boats. They raced to find him. They called his name: "Qu Yuan! Qu Yuan!"
They also threw food into the water — sticky rice dumplings. They wanted the fish to eat the food... not Qu Yuan.
Today, every year, we do the same things!
We race dragon boats. We eat zongzi. We hang wormwood on our doors. We remember Qu Yuan.

[Ending]
Qu Yuan gave his life for what he believed in.
In China, we have a saying: "We never forget the people who love our country."
This is our story. This is the Dragon Boat Festival. Thank you!
```
