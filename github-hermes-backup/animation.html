Who Was Qu Yuan?

  • More than 2,000 years ago, there lived a great man in China.
  • His name wasQu Yuan — a poet and a minister.
  • He loved his country — the Kingdom of Chu — very much.
  • He wrote beautiful poems. People still read them today.
  • He was very, very smart. People respected him.
📜 Poet 🇨🇳 Patriotic 🏛️ Minister

汨罗江 · Miluo River
One morning, Qu Yuan walked to the Miluo River.
He was 62 years old.
He looked at the water... and he jumped in.
"He jumped into the river. He gave his life for what he believed in."

How Do We Remember Him?

🚣

Dragon Boat Races

People raced boats to find him, calling his name!

"We race dragon boats every year."
🍃

Eat Zongzi

Sticky rice dumplings — so fish would not hurt him.

"We eat zongzi in his memory."
🌿

Hang Wormwood

People hang it on doors to keep bad luck away.

"We hang wormwood on our doors."
